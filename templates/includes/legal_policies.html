Privacy Policy

How we collect and use personal data

Last updated: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MobileFix Pro (“we”) handles personal information when you use our digital SaaS product, in line with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the SPDI Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (as applicable).

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: shop name, address, phone, email, password (stored hashed), logo, GSTIN, and GST state.
  • Usage data: login time, IP address, device/browser information for security. Sessions keep you logged in.
  • Shop operational data you enter: customer names, phones, device details (including IMEI/serial), job and invoice records, inventory, HSN/SAC, GST tax slabs, GST on sales and purchases, GSTR-1 / GSTR-2 style report data, and optional ID details you choose to store for compliance (including Aadhaar number on FRP/flash jobs).
  • Smart Tools ID files: PDFs or photos you open in e-Aadhaar or Document Print Pack (PAN, voter ID, driving licence, Aadhaar) are processed in your browser for PVC/A4 print. We do not receive those files on our servers.
  • IMEI skip PIN: if IMEI capture skip is enabled for a shop, a PIN is stored so staff can skip IMEI capture. After it is set, the PIN is not shown again in the browser.
  • Payment data: we do not store full card numbers. Payment status, amount, and gateway reference may be stored. Card/UPI credentials are handled by the payment gateway.

2. Why we use data

  • To create and run your shop account and subscription.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To provide support, send service messages, and improve the product.
  • To run GST billing and GSTR-1 / GSTR-2 style drafts for your shop (not tax advice).
  • For platform admin support: IMEI/serial search across shops (fraud and device tracing) and signed-in “open as shop” help. That shop access is logged.
  • To meet legal, tax, and accounting duties.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:

  • Payment gateway and banking partners to complete a transaction.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers who process data on our instructions.
  • WhatsApp on the user’s own device: job-card text and similar shop messages may be opened in WhatsApp for the shop to send. Admin IMEI-search WhatsApp uses a mobile number typed at send time (the field starts empty; it is not auto-filled from the customer).
  • MobileFix Pro platform admin, for IMEI search, fraud/device tracing, and logged “open as shop” support.
  • Authorities when required by law.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on secured servers. Passwords are hashed. We use access controls, HTTPS, and sessions to keep you logged in. GSTIN, IMEI, job, and customer data are stored for the shop that entered them. Each shop’s jobs, customers, and inventory belong to that shop; platform admin access for IMEI search and support is the exception. The IMEI skip PIN is not returned to the browser after it is set. No method of transmission is 100% secure; you must also protect your login and devices.
